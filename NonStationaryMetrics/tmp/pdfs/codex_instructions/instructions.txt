   Instructions for Codex – CQG Editorial Review
Goal. Read paper1-11.pdf as a CQG Associate Editor. Produce an editorial review, not a
mathematical referee report.

Workflow.
1. Read the manuscript completely.
2. Save intermediate notes (Markdown or LaTeX) for: Abstract, Title, Keywords,
Introduction, Literature, Formalism, Applications, Figures, Conclusions, Bibliography.
3. Produce a final 20–30 page editorial report based on those notes.

Include.
Executive summary; simulated editorial decision; strengths; weaknesses;
section-by-section comments; literature positioning; scientific narrative; figure
assessment; notation consistency; major/minor revision checklist; final
recommendation.

Constraints.
Do not invent new science. Do not rewrite the mathematics unless needed for clarity.
Focus on editorial quality, readability, positioning within the literature and likelihood of
passing CQG desk review.
