Abstract
The aim of the present paper is to study the geodesics paths of manifold from
metrizable spaces with spherically-symmetric weight tensors.
The Ricci flow of the Generalized-Schwarzschild spacetimes is newly analyzed.
The soliton configurations are taken as trivial Ricci soliton of (Generalized)-
Schwarzschild spacetimes and as complete gradient Ricci solitons.
The Euler-Lagrange approach is taken in the case of manifolds with spherically-
symmetric weights.
The geodesics paths are this way revealed.
The curvature estimates are adapted to gradient shrinking Ricci solitons.
The Lopez-Rio rigidity of gradient shrinking Kaehler and to gradient shrinking
Ricci solitons; the implications of harmonic Weyl tensor are discussed.
The results apply directly to gradient steady Ricci solitons.



1    Introduction
In the work of Ni et a. [17], the results of Perelman [23] about gradient shrinking
solitons are scrutinized.
The κ non-collapsing assumption is generalized in dim = 3.
As a further result, ibidem, the gradient shrinking solitons with vanishing Weyl
curvature are generalized. The rotationally-symmetric solitons are compre-
hended within the demonstration.
It is the aim of the present analysis to provide with new curvature estimates
of gradient shrinking solitons with spherically-symmetric weight. The cases of
solitons with vanishing Schouten tensor is newly naturally included.
The present paper newly expands the results of [18]according to which the gradi-
ent shrinking solitons on rotationally-symmetric-metric are made to correspond


                                        1
to R × Sn−1 on a cylindrical metric; more in detail, in the present analysis, the
result is newly extended to weighted manifolds with cylindrical metrics.
The present paper newly extends also the result of Perelman [23] according to
which ’tho only complete shrinking solitons whose sectional curvature is non-
negative should be the flat metric R3 . In the here-presented extensions, nev-
ertheless, weighted manifolds with tensor weights are newly proven to be Ricci
solitons with vanishing Weyl curvature and with vanishing Schouten tensor: the
case of spherically-symmetric weights is newly implemented as a particular case.
It is our of the aims of the present paper to further extended the results from
[17] about [23] to at least 4 spacetime dimensions.
As a further aim, the implications of rotationally-symmetric solitons are as-
cribed within a new paradigm of spherically-symmetric vector-weight solitons,
where, for the latter, the rotationally-symmetric soliton are framed within the
broader formalisms of spherically-symmetric weights.
The present paper represent and advance with respect to the approach of [29],
in which the spatial part of the Einstein solitons could be discussed as α cosym-
plectic manifold, and whose connected-ness was to be discussed.
The prescriptions of [28] are here followed, according to which the matter is never
put in the metric tensor, not even for the backreaction in the ultra-Relativistic
limit.
In the present paper, the Euler-Lagrange approach of Schwarzschild solitons is
applied in Sections 3-7; the remaining Sections are aimed at proving the well-
posed-ness of the procedures. Accordingly, the geodesics can be discussed.
The paper is organized as follows.
In Section 2, the notation is worked out.
In Section 3, the Euler-Lagrange approach of Schwarzschild solitons is applied.
In Section 4, the energies (i.e. those from the Hamiltonian analysis) are dis-
cussed in particular cases.
In Section 5, the weights are discussed.
In Section 6, the total energy per unit mass is discerned.
In Section 7, the geodesics paths are demonstrated to be obtained.
In Section 8, some new majorization theorems are presented.
In Section 9, comparison with previous literature is achieved, and new theorems
are accomplished.
In Section 10, further comparisons are provided with, form which further new
thoerems are developped.
In Section 11, the outlook is presented.
