                                  Introduction.

   The harmonic morphisms of a Riemannian manifold, M ,
into another, N , are the morphisms for the harmonic struc-
tures on M and N (the harmonic functions on a Riemannian
manifold being those which satisfy the Laplace-Beltrami
equation). These morphisms were introduced and studied by
Constantinescu and Cornea [4] in the more general frame of
harmonic spaces, as a natural generalization of the conformal
mappings between Riemann surfaces (1). See also Sibony [14].
   The present paper deals with harmonic morphisms between
two Riemannian manifolds M and N of arbitrary (not
necessarily equal) dimensions. It turns out, however, that if
dim M < dim N , the only harmonic morphisms M -> N
are the constant mappings. Thus we are left with the case
                                dim M ^ dim N .
If dim N = 1 , say N = R , the harmonic morphisms
M —> R are nothing but the harmonic functions on M .

surfaces, then it is known that the harmonic morphisms of
