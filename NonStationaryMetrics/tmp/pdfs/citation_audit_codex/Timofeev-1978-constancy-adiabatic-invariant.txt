On the constancy of an adiabatic invariant when the
nature of the motion changes
         A. V. Timofeev
         (Submitted 28 April 1978)
         Zh. Eksp. Teor. Fi.75, 1303-1308 (October 1978)

         The motion of a charged particle in a spatially periodic field with amplitude increasing with the time is
         considered. The change of an adiabatic invariant when the particle is captured by a wave is calculated.
         The expressions obtained can also be used to describe the motion of a pendulum of variable length in a
         gravitational field as it goes from rotation to vibration about a position of stable equilibrium.
