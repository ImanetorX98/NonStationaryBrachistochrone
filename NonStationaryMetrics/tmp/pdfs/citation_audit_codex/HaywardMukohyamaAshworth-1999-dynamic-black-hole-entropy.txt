Abstract

   We consider two non-statistical definitions of the entropy of dynamic Žnon-stationary. black holes in a spherical
symmetry. The first is analogous to the original Clausius definition of thermodynamic entropy: there is a first law containing
an energy-supply term which equals surface gravity times a total differential. The second is Wald’s Noether-charge method,
adapted to dynamic black holes by using the Kodama flow. Both definitions give the same value for the Einstein gravity:
one-quarter the area of the trapping horizon. q 1999 Elsevier Science B.V. All rights reserved.
