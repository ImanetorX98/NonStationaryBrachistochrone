\noindent Dear Editors,

\vspace{0.5em}
I am submitting a revised version of manuscript \textbf{CQG-116884}, now titled
\textbf{``Brachistochrones in conformal Kerr spacetimes: control domain, exterior
separatrices, and adiabatic response''}. The title has changed because the result it
previously advertised does not survive: the referee showed that the ergosphere
trichotomy was contradicted by our own appendix, and the corrected statement is a
four-case classification. I am grateful for a report that was specific enough to be
acted on.

The revision follows the referee's recommended reconstruction rather than patching
locally. The exterior retrograde separatrix at $r_d\simeq3.5139M$ is now the flagship
exact result, as suggested. A new section proves the compactness criterion the report
sketched and derives from it that the compact-control problem \emph{ends} at $r=2M$
for the selector $W=\partial_\eta$; a three-way protocol---exterior extremal, limiting
contact, analytic continuation with no optimality claimed---is applied without
exception, including in the figures. The extremal-versus-minimiser statements are
corrected, and the claim that numerical perturbation establishes minimality is
removed. The $J=-J_c$ cusp/corner contradiction is resolved by rederiving from the
Hamilton equations: \emph{both} printed statements were wrong, and every affected
figure, table and conclusion has been regenerated.

The reproducibility findings were all correct and are all fixed. The absent module is
restored, the archive is dependency-complete from a fresh clone, and the printed
slopes and checksums are now generated by one archived command rather than
transcribed. The provenance record carries a content digest that excludes wall-clock
timings, so an independent run reproduces it exactly
(DOI: \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22110181}{10.5281/zenodo.22110181}).

Two further changes go beyond the reports. Every source underpinning a claim was read
at first hand, which produced thirty corrections---among them a priority distinction
we had missed, and a circular appeal to a definition quoted as a theorem. And the
request for experimental validation is now answered at three levels rather than
declined: bench emulation of the reduced controlled system, analogue platforms that
already realise the ergosurface and the time-dependent scale factor, and
gravitational validation, which remains unavailable and is not claimed.

This manuscript is the second of a \textbf{two-part companion series}. Its shared
control-theoretic foundations are proved in the companion paper, \emph{``Controlled-rail
brachistochrones in non-stationary spacetimes: conformal symmetry, Kodama energy, and
Vaidya dynamics''}
(DOI: \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22110568}{10.5281/zenodo.22110568}), which
is under consideration at the \emph{Journal of Mathematical Physics}. That paper has
also been revised---the incorrect statement about Pontryagin extremals identified in
this review was present there too---and the deposited version above is the corrected
one. The present manuscript
(DOI: \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22110584}{10.5281/zenodo.22110584}) cites
those results without re-deriving them.

I confirm that this manuscript is \textbf{original}, has \textbf{not been published}
and is \textbf{not under consideration for publication elsewhere}, and that its
submission is approved by the author. There are \textbf{no competing interests} and the
research received \textbf{no external funding}. In line with IOP's policy on
generative-AI tools, the Acknowledgements disclose the AI assistants used for
symbolic-algebra checks, numerical-code development and language editing; all
AI-assisted output was independently verified by the author, who takes full
responsibility for the accuracy, originality and integrity of the work.

Thank you for considering this submission. I look forward to the reviewers' comments.
