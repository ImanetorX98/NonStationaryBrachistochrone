\noindent Dear Editors,

\vspace{0.5em}
I am pleased to submit the manuscript \textbf{``Brachistochrones in conformal Kerr
spacetimes: ergosphere trichotomy, separatrices, and adiabatic response''} for
consideration as a research article in \emph{Classical and Quantum Gravity}.

The paper develops the controlled-rail brachistochrone---the fastest constrained worldline
reaching a fixed spatial target at a free arrival clock---in the rotating, axisymmetric
\textbf{Thakurta--Kerr} spacetime, the conformal Kerr geometry $g=A(\eta)^2 g_{\rm Kerr}$,
where frame dragging, an ergosphere and genus-two spectral curves all appear. Its main
contributions are: (i)~the \textbf{breathing-indicatrix Hamiltonians} and the
conformal-time brachistochrone, with the arrival branch $t\equiv\eta$ distinct from proper
time $\tau$; (ii)~\textbf{closed-form equatorial separatrices} in Weierstrass functions,
continued through the ergosphere in Doran coordinates, with a trichotomy and an analytic
cusp theorem; (iii)~the rotational and conformal \textbf{plunge inversion}, with an
existence theorem for the conformal inversion; and (iv)~a first-order \textbf{adiabatic
correction} (on-shell and off-shell) verified against the true non-autonomous flow, with
weight-two content a length-two iterated Abelian integral on the genus-two curve.

The work fits the scope of \emph{Classical and Quantum Gravity}, combining rotating
black-hole optics, ergosphere and frame-dragging phenomenology, optical (Randers--Zermelo)
geometry and exact hyperelliptic techniques, and isolating how frame dragging, time
dependence and branch-dependent cost functionals compete within one variational framework.
Every closed form is derived symbolically
and verified numerically; the full reproducibility package is openly archived on Zenodo
(DOI: \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21707378}{10.5281/zenodo.21707378}).

This manuscript is the second of a \textbf{two-part companion series}. Its shared
control-theoretic foundations---the controlled-rail principle, existence and normality, the
non-autonomous Hamilton--Jacobi verification criterion, and the
Killing\,/\,conformal-Killing\,/\,Kodama selector hierarchy---are proved in the companion
paper, \emph{``Controlled-rail brachistochrones in non-stationary spacetimes: conformal
symmetry, Kodama energy, and Vaidya dynamics''}
(DOI: \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21781850}{10.5281/zenodo.21781850}), submitted
to \emph{Classical and Quantum Gravity} concurrently; the present paper
(DOI: \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21740000}{10.5281/zenodo.21740000}) cites those
results without re-deriving them and develops the rotating conformal-Kerr application in
full. I should be grateful if the two submissions could receive \textbf{coordinated
editorial handling}.
