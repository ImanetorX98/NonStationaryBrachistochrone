# Editorial notes — Literature positioning

## Current coverage

The paper cites Perlick, Kovner and the Randers/Zermelo literature; Pontryagin and a modern control text; standard Kodama, Misner–Sharp, dynamical-horizon and Vaidya sources; and a substantial special-function bibliography. These are good pillars.

## Essential missing cluster

The introduction should engage the direct relativistic-brachistochrone sequence, not only Perlick (1991):

- F. Giannoni, P. Piccione and J. A. Verderesi, “An approach to the relativistic brachistochrone problem by sub-Riemannian geometry,” *J. Math. Phys.* 38 (1997) 6367–6381.
- F. Giannoni and P. Piccione, “An existence theory for relativistic brachistochrones in stationary space-times,” *J. Math. Phys.* 39 (1998) 6137–6152.
- F. Giannoni, P. Piccione and D. V. Tausk, “Morse theory for the travel time brachistochrones in stationary spacetimes,” *DCDS* 8 (2002) 697–724, DOI 10.3934/dcds.2002.8.697.
- F. Giannoni and P. Piccione, “The arrival time brachistochrones in general relativity,” *J. Geom. Anal.* 12 (2002) 375–423, DOI 10.1007/BF02922047.

These papers are not merely background decoration. They use observer fields, conserved laws, endpoint worldlines, travel/arrival-time distinctions, existence and multiplicity. The present paper must explain exactly how the controlled-rail admissible set and the non-stationary selector differ.

## Current-literature check

Two recent preprints are relevant enough to consider before submission:

- Caponio and Javaloyes (2026), a survey of formulations and extensions of Fermat's principle in general relativity; it is useful for terminology and for avoiding an overbroad historical claim.
- arXiv:2607.29265, “The brachistochrone problem for a constant velocity traveler in static and spherically symmetric spacetimes.” It is very recent and stationary, but its constant-local-speed protocol is close enough that the paper should either cite it or explain why fixed local speed and fixed controlled Kodama/Killing charge define different problems.

A December 2025 preprint on brachistochrone-ruled timelike surfaces is adjacent but not essential unless the introduction expands its discussion of stationary Jacobi/Finsler reductions.

## Positioning sentence to aim for

The manuscript should say, in substance: previous work gives stationary variational, sub-Riemannian and global-existence theories (and general Fermat principles for geodesic arrival problems); the present contribution is the controlled maintenance of a selected timelike charge in a non-autonomous background, with Kodama selection and explicit dynamical examples.

## Special-function literature

The higher-genus references are extensive and mostly appropriate. However, the paper must separate established named structures from descriptive terminology. “Genus-two dilogarithm” and “hyperelliptic polylogarithm” should be introduced as representations/classes used here, not as universally standard single objects, and conjectural irreducibility must remain visibly labelled as conjectural.
