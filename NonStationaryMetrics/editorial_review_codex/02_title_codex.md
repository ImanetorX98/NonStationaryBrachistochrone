# Editorial notes — Title

Current title: **Controlled-rail brachistochrones in non-stationary spacetimes: conformal symmetry, Kodama energy, and Vaidya dynamics**.

## Assessment

The title is accurate and rich in search terms, but it is long and advertises three conceptual centres. “Conformal symmetry” points partly toward Paper II, “Kodama energy” names the distinctive Vaidya mechanism, and “Vaidya dynamics” names the application. Together they make the manuscript sound broader and more diffuse than its best Paper-I story.

The unusual phrase “controlled-rail brachistochrones” is legitimate, but a reader outside the immediate variational-geometry niche will not yet know whether this means a physical guide, an imposed velocity constraint, or an optimal-control actuator. The title can carry the phrase if the abstract explains it immediately.

## Options

1. **Time-optimal controlled worldlines in FLRW and Vaidya spacetimes** — clearest and most application-led.
2. **Controlled-rail brachistochrones in dynamical spacetimes: from conformal symmetry to Kodama flow** — preserves the conceptual ladder.
3. **Relativistic brachistochrones with controlled Kodama energy** — most focused, but underplays the general formalism and FLRW.

Editorial preference: option 1 if the manuscript is shortened around its physical applications; option 2 if the hierarchy remains the organising principle. The current title is publishable but not optimal.

## Terminology

Choose one house form and use it everywhere: IOP normally accepts “non-stationary”, but “dynamical spacetime” is shorter and more natural in the gravitational context. Do not alternate “non-stationary”, “time-dependent”, “evolving” and “dynamical” as if they were different technical classes unless the distinction is intended.
