## Testi modello per Paper II (§7, inserire alle aperture)
- **7.1 apertura formalismo**: "We use the controlled-rail variational problem established in Paper I. On a
  regular domain where the selector is timelike and the velocity indicatrix is smooth, compact and uniformly
  convex, every minimizer is a normal Pontryagin extremal. The arrival clock is free, hence H(s_f)=0; global
  optimality requires the non-autonomous Hamilton-Jacobi verification criterion of Paper I. We now specialize
  this construction to the conformal Kerr geometry."
- **7.2 apertura riduzione adiabatica**: "By Lemma I.4 of Paper I, every source of the form
  ∂_λ F = N_λ/S^{3/2} reduces uniquely to the Abelian basis through the degree-ten polynomial identity. For
  Thakurta-Kerr the slow parameter is A and the source is generated by E_eff ∂_Eeff + J_eff ∂_Jeff. The
  resulting coefficients are given below."
- **7.3 apertura appendice off-shell**: "The complete first-order response follows from Theorem I.5 of Paper I.
  The new feature of the conformal Kerr application is that the normalization P_r=p_r/A is time-dependent and
  non-canonical; passing from the canonical pair (r,p_r) to P_r produces the dilation term -(A'/A)P_r. We
  derive this metric-specific contribution and its Abelian reduction here."
- **7.4 ladder pointer**: "Paper I established the indicatrix ladder FLRW circle -> Vaidya radial-wind ellipse
  -> conformal-Kerr azimuthal-wind ellipse. The present paper develops the last member, where frame dragging
  shifts the centre azimuthally and the conformal factor makes the semi-axes breathe."
