\vspace{0.5em}
I am pleased to submit the manuscript \textbf{``Controlled-rail brachistochrones in
non-stationary spacetimes: conformal symmetry, Kodama energy, and Vaidya dynamics''} for
consideration as a research article in \emph{General Relativity and Gravitation}.

The paper extends the relativistic brachistochrone---the fastest constrained ``rail''
worldline reaching a fixed spatial target at a free arrival clock---from stationary to
genuinely \textbf{non-stationary, spherically symmetric spacetimes}: spatially flat FLRW
and the ingoing Vaidya dynamical black hole. Its central contributions are: (i)~a
\textbf{controlled-rail variational formulation} in which the actively maintained
invariant $-u\cdot W=\hat E$ is legitimised through Pontryagin's principle, with the
selector $W$ following a Killing $\to$ conformal-Killing $\to$ \textbf{Kodama} hierarchy;
(ii)~an \textbf{existence and normality theorem} together with a \textbf{non-autonomous
Hamilton--Jacobi verification criterion} appropriate to time-dependent geometries; and
(iii)~a first-order \textbf{adiabatic correction that closes in a horizon dilogarithm},
verified against the true non-autonomous flow, whose off-shell source admits the universal
boundary form $S_{\mathcal D}=[\,r\,p_r\,]-\lambda$.

The work sits squarely within the scope of \emph{General Relativity and Gravitation}: it
combines dynamical black-hole optics, optical (Randers--Zermelo) geometry, non-autonomous
Hamiltonian and optimal-control methods, and exact special-function techniques, and it
makes precise and computable the distinction between effects generated by homogeneous
cosmological expansion and those generated by radial mass flow. Every closed form is
derived symbolically and verified numerically; the full reproducibility package is openly
archived on Zenodo
(DOI: \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21782443}{10.5281/zenodo.21782443}), and a
preprint of the present paper is archived at
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21781850}{10.5281/zenodo.21781850}.

This manuscript is the \textbf{self-contained} first part of a two-part companion series;
it establishes the general formalism and its spherically symmetric (FLRW and Vaidya)
realisations. The rotating, axisymmetric conformal-Kerr (Thakurta--Kerr) application is
developed in a \emph{distinct} companion manuscript, \emph{``Brachistochrones in conformal
Kerr spacetimes: ergosphere trichotomy, separatrices, and adiabatic response''}
(Zenodo DOI: \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21740000}{10.5281/zenodo.21740000}),
which is currently under consideration at \emph{Classical and Quantum Gravity}. I have
uploaded its present version as a related file, because it illustrates the physical
consequences of the results proved here; the two papers share authorship but not content,
and the present submission can be assessed entirely on its own.

published} and is \textbf{not under consideration for publication elsewhere}, and that its
